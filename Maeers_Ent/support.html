FAQs

  • Can you play interrupts at anytime?
    Yes, you can play interrupts at anytime during the game.
  • When playing a field spell does it destory your opponents field spell?
    No. Thier field spell stays on the field when you play a field spell.
  • What happens if I have no pages left in my grimore and I can't draw?
    The player that has no pages left in thier grimore loses the game.
  • How are leaderboards calculated?
    Times are recorded when you complete the full speedrun.
  • My run didn’t save. What do I do?
    Check your connection, then restart the game and try another run. If it keeps happening, contact us below.
  • Can I use a controller?
    No. Reboot does not support controller.
  • How do I report a bug?
    Use the contact form below and include as many details as possible (platform, steps to reproduce, screenshots).
  • Where can I give feedback?
    Join our community discord or send feedback directly through the form below.
  • Do you offer refunds?
    Refunds are handled through the store where you purchased the game, subject to their policy.
