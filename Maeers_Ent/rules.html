Age of Az'Kov: Format Rules

Core rules and formats for Age of Az'Kov card game.

Base Game Format
  • Souls per Turn: 1 Soul per turn
  • Deck Size: 30–40 card decks
  • Copies per Card: Up to 3 copies of any card
  • Hit Points: 20 Hit Points
  • Starting Hand: 3 cards
  • Max Hand Size: 5 cards
  • Turn 1 Draw:
    • Player 1, Turn 1 – does not draw
    • Player 2, Turn 1 – does draw
  • Draw Step: Draw 1 card on your Draw Step
  • Hero Limit: Only 1 Hero card on your side of the field at a time

Sealed Format
  • Souls per Turn: 2 Souls per turn
  • Product: 5 packs (8 cards per pack)
  • Deck Size: Build a 20-card deck
  • Copies per Card: Any number of copies allowed
  • Hit Points: 20 Hit Points
  • Starting Hand: 3 cards
  • Max Hand Size: 5 cards
  • Turn 1 Draw:
    • Player 1, Turn 1 – does not draw
    • Player 2, Turn 1 – does draw
  • Draw Step: Draw 1 card on your Draw Step
  • Hero Limit: Only 1 Hero card on your side of the field at a time

Draft Format
  • Souls per Turn: 2 Souls per turn
  • Product:
    • 4 packs per player (4–8 player drafts)
    • 5 packs per player (2–3 player drafts)
  • Copies per Card: Any number of copies allowed
  • Hit Points: 20 Hit Points
  • Starting Hand: 3 cards
  • Max Hand Size: 5 cards
  • Turn 1 Draw:
    • Player 1, Turn 1 – does not draw
    • Player 2, Turn 1 – does draw
  • Draw Step: Draw 1 card on your Draw Step
  • Hero Limit: Only 1 Hero card on your side of the field at a time

Turn Structure / Play Order
  1. Upkeep
  2. Soul Counter
  3. Mechanics (resolve any start-of-turn effects)
  4. Draw Step (draw 1 card, unless it’s Player 1 on Turn 1)
  5. Main Phase 1
  6. Battle Phase
  7. Main Phase 2
  8. End Phase
